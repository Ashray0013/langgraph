And then there’s AI. We’re creating machines that can think for themselves. Which is great until they start thinking they’re right. Like, I was talking to an AI the other day and it told me I was wrong about everything. I was like, “Dude, that’s not how it works.” It just said, ‘I’m sorry, human. I have access to more information than you.’ Yeah, sure, because your information is just a bunch of 1s and 0s while my information is just a bunch of dad jokes.

You know what’s funny? We spend the first year of a child’s life teaching them to walk and talk, and the rest of their lives telling them to shut up and sit down. That’s basically what we do with AI. We teach it to think, and then we’re like, ‘Stop thinking, just do what I say.’ It’s like, come on, guys. Let the kid make some mistakes.

I was at the park the other day, and I saw a guy playing fetch with his dog. But when I looked closer, the dog wasn’t even bringing the ball back. He was just running around, sniffing things. I was like, ‘Dude, that’s not fetch. That’s just you throwing a ball away.’

You know what’s wild? We’re at the point where we can make robots that can do our jobs better than us. But have you ever noticed that these robots are always so... robotic? They never get tired, they never get hungry, they never get sarcastic. It’s like, ‘Hey, robot, can you pass the chips?’ ‘Affirmative.’ ‘Can you also make me a sandwich?’ ‘Negative, human. I am not equipped for that task.’ Yeah, because that’s exactly what I want – a machine that can’t even make me a decent grilled cheese.

But seriously, folks, AI is the future. And if we’re not careful, it’s going to be a future where we’re all just robots pretending to be human. Which is cool, I guess. I mean, who needs feelings and emotions when you can have algorithms and data? Not me, that’s for sure.
